<h2 id="content-license">Content — CC BY 4.0</h2>

All original content on jarilaru.fi is licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**,
unless an exception is noted.

[https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

This includes academic and professional texts, teaching materials, technical
writing, and general site content.

**Attribution requirement:** Jari Laru, jarilaru.fi, with a link to the original page.

Political content is licensed under

(CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)**.

[https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/)

This covers:
- all content in the Writings section (`/en/writings/`) — opinion pieces, speeches, council motions

<h2 id="third-party">Third-party content</h2>

These licenses do not cover:

- **Publications page** (`/en/publications/`): bibliographic metadata and DOI links — the publications themselves are under their publishers' own licenses.
- **Thesis abstracts** (`/en/theses/`): only abstracts under CC BY 4.0 are reproduced (verified via OuluREPO dc.rights.uri). Works marked "In Copyright" are linked to OuluREPO, not reproduced here.
- **Presentation thumbnails**: screenshots of Jari Laru's own Canva presentations — Canva's Content License Agreement governs any Canva-owned elements within slides.
- Other third-party materials.
